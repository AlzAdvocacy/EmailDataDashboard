Quorum sent email performance

Alzheimer’s Association advocacy outbox · June 2, 2025 – September 15, 2026 · 1,099 sends, 1,802,707 recipients, 71 senders

Read first Five sends are pulling the program average down

  • Five sends went to the full advocate file of roughly 125,000 people: one on June 10, 2025 and four in the week of November 21, 2025. Together they are 626,793 recipients, 34.8% of everyone reached in 16 months, and they opened at 10.4% because a full-file blast reaches every lapsed address on the list, not just engaged advocates. The other 1,094 sends opened at 20.6%. That single difference is what makes the program-wide open rate 17.0%, and it is why the typical individual send (median 22.6%) looks so much better than the program average.
  • Two sets of sends are left out by default. Twenty-seven sends from Sept 13–15, 2026 were still accruing opens and unsubscribes when this was pulled, and are excluded via the toggle below. Nine A/B parent records from May 19, 2026 (105,658 recipients) are excluded permanently: they carry no statistics at all because the results live on variant records this pull does not include.
  • Bounce rate here is one number, not a diagnosis. Quorum’s list view returns a single bounce count with no hard, soft or block split, so read the bounce chart as a hygiene signal and pull the individual email for anything that looks wrong.

Method and definitions

Where the data came from

Pulled live from the Quorum outbox list API (/api/bulkemail/) on Sept 15, 2026, filtered to sent_datetime ≥ 2025-06-01, excluding drafts, scheduled sends, archived items and A/B variant children. That returned 1,108 records; 9 A/B parent records with no statistics were removed, leaving 1,099 sends spanning 2025-06-02 15:25 to 2026-09-15 14:23 Eastern. All IDs unique.

How each rate is calculated

  • Open rate = opens ÷ (recipients − bounces), using the open count Quorum reports. Median open rate is the middle value across individual sends, unweighted by size.
  • Click rate = unique clicks ÷ delivered. CTOR = clicks ÷ opens.
  • Unsubscribe rate = unsubscribes ÷ delivered. Bounce rate = bounces ÷ recipients.
  • One definitional difference from Quorum’s own screen. Quorum’s % Unsubscribes column divides by recipients; this page divides by delivered. On a send with 467 recipients, 9 bounces and 1 unsubscribe, Quorum shows 0.21% and this page shows 0.22%.

How this was checked

The 242 sends that overlap with the earlier July–September pull were compared field by field: recipients, opens, clicks and bounces matched exactly on all 242. Unsubscribes differed on three sends, all from the previous 6 days, which is the live-data effect the maturity toggle exists for. Eight sends were also checked against what the Quorum outbox table itself displays. Row count, unique IDs and date bounds were confirmed against the API’s own total count. The transfer of the dataset was verified by checksum.
